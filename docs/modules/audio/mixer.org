:PROPERTIES:

=LrgAudioMixer= (=src/audio/lrg-audio-mixer.h=, GIR =Libregnum.AudioMixer=) is libregnum's PCM mixer graph: voices of =LrgWaveData= play into named buses; buses run their effects, are faded, muted, soloed and ducked, and add into their parent and their send targets until everything reaches =master=. It renders offline (=lrg_audio_mixer_render()=, no window or audio device) or feeds a =GrlAudioStream= (=lrg_audio_mixer_pump()=); =LrgAudioManager= plays its mixer on the device by itself (=lrg_audio_manager_set_mixer()=). The game's layout normally comes from a [[file:mixer-asset.org][=.rmixer= file]].

It complements the audio manager's direct playback: =GrlSound= voices started with =lrg_audio_manager_play_sound()= are not routed through it ([[file:buses.org][LrgAudioBus]] covers those on the device mix). Sources that should go through the graph play through it: an [[file:../world3d/components.org::#audio-source][=LrgAudioSourceComponent=]] with a =bus= does.

** Concepts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts
:END:

*** Format and graph
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: graph
:END:
A mixer has a fixed sample rate (8,000 to 192,000 Hz, =lrg_audio_mixer_get_sample_rate()=), one or two channels (=lrg_audio_mixer_get_channels()=) and a =master= bus. =lrg_audio_mixer_add_bus()= adds a bus under an existing parent; parents are fixed at creation (=lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_parent()=), so a different tree needs a new mixer. Up to 256 buses including master. =lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_count()=, =lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_name()= (creation order, master is 0) and =lrg_audio_mixer_has_bus()= enumerate them.

Sends (=lrg_audio_mixer_set_send()=) copy a bus's post-fader output into another bus. The graph rejects cycles (through parents, sends or ducking rules), self-sends, sends from master and sends to a bus's own parent; gain 0 removes a send. =lrg_audio_mixer_get_send()= and =lrg_audio_mixer_list_sends()= read them back.

*** Signal flow
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: signal-flow
:END:
Each render is cut into blocks of =LRG_AUDIO_MIXER_BLOCK_FRAMES= (256). Per block:

1. effect-parameter ramps set their properties;
2. every voice is read into its bus: pitch (linear interpolation), its own one-pole low-pass, gain and balance (both ramped per frame);
3. buses are processed in dependency order: the built-in low-pass, the built-in feedback delay (=lrg_audio_mixer_set_effects()=), the bus's effect chain (=lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_chain()=), then per frame the ducking gain, the fader (=lrg_audio_mixer_set_bus()=, ramped per frame) and mute/solo; the result is metered and added to the parent and the send targets.

Master's output is clipped to [-1, 1]; intermediate buses keep headroom. Gains (bus, send, voice) are linear, 0 to 4 (+12 dB); =lrg_audio_db_to_gain()= and =lrg_audio_gain_to_db()= convert from and to decibels (=LRG_AUDIO_SILENCE_DB= is 0, =LRG_AUDIO_MIXER_MAX_GAIN_DB= is a gain of 4).

Mute silences a bus's output and sends while voices and effect histories keep advancing. Pausing a voice (=lrg_audio_mixer_pause_voice()=) freezes only its cursor; stopping it (=lrg_audio_mixer_stop_voice()=) leaves effect tails ringing. Finished one-shot voices are removed after their last sample, so =lrg_audio_mixer_get_voice_count()= of zero does not mean silent output while tails ring. Voice ids are never reused.

*** Effect chains
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: chains
:END:
=lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_chain()= returns the bus's [[file:effects.org][=LrgAudioEffectChain=]], created on first use. Add any =LrgAudioEffect= (reverb, low-pass, high-pass, echo, chorus, pitch shift or your own); it runs at the mixer's rate and channel count, after the built-in low-pass and delay and before ducking and the fader. Edit effects between renders, from the thread that renders.

*** Solo
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: solo
:END:
=lrg_audio_mixer_set_bus_solo()= / =lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_solo()=: while any bus is soloed only soloed buses, their children (recursively) and every bus downstream of them (parents and send targets, recursively) are heard; others output silence (their voices and effects keep running). =lrg_audio_mixer_is_bus_audible()= answers for one bus, mute included (mute wins over solo).

*** Ramps
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ramps
:END:
Snapshot blends (and fades in general) are ramps:

- =lrg_audio_mixer_ramp_bus_gain()= moves a fader linearly from its current gain to a target over a time of rendered audio, one step per frame;
- =lrg_audio_mixer_ramp_send()= does the same for a send (creating it at 0 when missing, with the same routing rules);
- =lrg_audio_mixer_ramp_effect_param()= ramps a numeric property (float, double, int, uint) of the effect at an index of a bus chain, set once per block; the effect's setter clamps.

A new ramp starts from wherever the running one has got to; =lrg_audio_mixer_set_bus()= and =lrg_audio_mixer_set_send()= cancel ramps. A zero time sets the value at once. =lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_gain()= returns the gain a ramp has reached; =lrg_audio_mixer_is_ramping()= tells whether any ramp is running. Ramps advance with rendered frames only, so offline renders are deterministic.

*** Ducking
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ducking
:END:
=lrg_audio_mixer_set_duck()= adds (or edits) a side-chain rule: when the trigger bus's per-frame output peak reaches =threshold_db=, the target bus's gain moves toward =depth_db=, otherwise toward 1, as a one-pole envelope with time constants =attack= and =release= (=g += (1 - exp (-1 / (seconds * rate))) (goal - g)=; 0 s jumps). The trigger renders before the target: a rule whose target feeds the trigger, or with master as trigger, is refused. Several rules on one target multiply. =lrg_audio_mixer_remove_duck()=, =lrg_audio_mixer_clear_ducks()= and =lrg_audio_mixer_get_duck_count()= manage them; =lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_duck_gain()= is the gain applied at the end of the last render (1 for none), for a gain-reduction meter.

*** Meters
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: meters
:END:
=lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_meter()= reports the peak (largest absolute sample) and RMS of a bus's output (after effects, ducking, fader, mute and solo: what reaches its parent) over the last =render()= or =pump()= call, per channel (0, 1) or for all channels (-1). Values are linear; =lrg_audio_gain_to_db()= converts for display. Master's meter is before the final clip.

*** Pull-source voices and unclipped output (0.10.0)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: source-voices
:END:
=lrg_audio_mixer_play_source (mixer, source, bus, gain)= plays an =LrgAudioSource= as a voice. A source is any object that renders its next frames when asked, such as an instrument (=LrgSynth=, =LrgDrumMachine=, =LrgSampler=) or your own GObject that implements the interface's =render= and =reset=. Every block, the mixer zeroes a scratch buffer, asks the source to add the block at the mixer's rate and channel count, then applies the voice gain and balance exactly as for a wave voice. =lrg_audio_mixer_set_voice_pitch()= and =lrg_audio_mixer_set_voice_lowpass()= return =FALSE= for a source voice. The voice ends once the source's =render= returns =FALSE= (the last block is still heard) or with =lrg_audio_mixer_stop_voice()=. The mixer holds a reference to the source while the voice exists. Non-finite samples from a source become silence.

=lrg_audio_mixer_set_clip (mixer, FALSE)= stops the clamp of the master output to [-1, 1]. Turn it off for offline renders that are measured and normalised afterwards, or when the master chain ends in an =LrgLimiter=, so that peaks are kept rather than flattened.

*** Spatial voice controls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: voice-controls
:END:
A voice can be steered while it plays, which is what spatial sources do every frame:

| Function | Effect |
|----------+--------|
| =lrg_audio_mixer_set_voice_gain()= | gain 0 .. 4, ramped linearly over a time (avoid zipper noise: ramp over the update interval) |
| =lrg_audio_mixer_set_voice_pan()= | balance -1 .. 1 in a stereo mixer: the far channel is scaled by =1 - abs(pan)=, a centred voice is unchanged; ramped like the gain |
| =lrg_audio_mixer_set_voice_pitch()= | playback rate 0.125 .. 8 with linear interpolation (doppler); 1 reads the samples exactly |
| =lrg_audio_mixer_set_voice_lowpass()= | a one-pole low-pass on the voice (occlusion); 0 turns it off |
| =lrg_audio_mixer_get_voice_bus()= / =lrg_audio_mixer_has_voice()= | where it plays; whether it still exists |

** Examples
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: examples
:END:

*** Routing, sends and an effect chain
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-routing
:END:
#+begin_src c
g_autoptr(LrgAudioMixer) mixer = lrg_audio_mixer_new (48000, 2);
g_autoptr(LrgReverb)     verb = lrg_reverb_new ();

lrg_audio_mixer_add_bus (mixer, "sfx", "master");
lrg_audio_mixer_add_bus (mixer, "weapons", "sfx");
lrg_audio_mixer_add_bus (mixer, "echo", "master");
lrg_audio_mixer_set_bus (mixer, "sfx", lrg_audio_db_to_gain (-6.0), FALSE);
lrg_audio_mixer_set_send (mixer, "weapons", "echo", lrg_audio_db_to_gain (-12.0));
lrg_reverb_set_preset (verb, LRG_REVERB_PRESET_HALL);
lrg_audio_effect_chain_add (lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_chain (mixer, "echo"), LRG_AUDIO_EFFECT (verb));

/* wave is an LrgWaveData loaded or generated by the application */
lrg_audio_mixer_play (mixer, wave, "weapons", 1.0, FALSE);
#+end_src

*** Ducking music under dialogue, with a meter
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-ducking
:END:
#+begin_src c
gdouble peak = 0.0, rms = 0.0;

lrg_audio_mixer_add_bus (mixer, "music", "master");
lrg_audio_mixer_add_bus (mixer, "dialogue", "master");
lrg_audio_mixer_set_duck (mixer, "dialogue", "music", -30.0, -12.0, 0.05, 0.4);

/* after each render or pump */
lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_meter (mixer, "music", -1, &peak, &rms);
g_print ("music %.1f dB peak, %.1f dB RMS, ducked %.1f dB\n", lrg_audio_gain_to_db (peak),
         lrg_audio_gain_to_db (rms), lrg_audio_gain_to_db (lrg_audio_mixer_get_bus_duck_gain (mixer, "music")));
#+end_src

*** Offline rendering
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-offline
:END:
#+begin_src c
gsize                  n_samples = 0;
g_autofree gfloat     *pcm = lrg_audio_mixer_render (mixer, 1024, &n_samples);
g_autoptr(LrgWaveData) output = NULL;

output = lrg_wave_data_new_from_samples (48000, 32, 2, (const guint8 *)pcm, n_samples * sizeof (gfloat));
lrg_wave_data_export_wav (output, "mixed.wav", &error);
#+end_src

Each render accepts 1 to 65,536 frames and advances the graph by exactly those frames; block boundaries do not reset effects, ramps or looping voices. The result counts interleaved *samples* (stereo has twice as many as frames).

*** Live stream integration
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-stream
:END:
=lrg_audio_manager_set_mixer()= does this for you (a 32-bit stream of =LRG_AUDIO_MANAGER_MIXER_BLOCK= frames, refilled from =lrg_audio_manager_update()= once a device is open). By hand:

#+begin_src c
g_autoptr(GrlAudioStream) stream = NULL;

grl_audio_stream_set_default_buffer_size (2048);
stream = grl_audio_stream_new (48000, 32, 2);
if (stream != NULL && grl_audio_stream_is_valid (stream))
{
	grl_audio_stream_play (stream);
	/* once per host frame while playing: */
	lrg_audio_mixer_pump (mixer, stream, 2048, &error);
}
#+end_src

The pump does nothing while the stream is stopped or paused or needs no refill; a format mismatch returns =G_IO_ERROR_INVALID_ARGUMENT= without advancing the graph. Choose a block size that fits the stream buffer.

** Performance
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: performance
:END:
Rendering 2048 frames at 48 kHz stereo through 4 buses with 32 looping voices, a low-pass, a send-fed reverb and a ducking rule takes about 0.39 ms on an i7-1280P core (=taskset -c 2=, =test-mixer-asset -m perf=). Plain voices (pitch 1, no voice filter, centred, no running ramp) take a copy-and-scale path; spatial voices take the per-frame path, roughly ten times the cost per voice. Bus effect chains run in float on a scratch block; the per-bus mix, the effect block and pump()'s output are kept between renders, so steady-state rendering does not allocate (=render()= returns a new buffer).

** Pitfalls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pitfalls
:END:
- One thread: graph edits, voice operations, rendering and pumping must all happen on the same thread; the mixer is meant for main-thread stream refills and offline export, not an audio-device callback.
- Parents are fixed; a changed tree needs a new mixer (=lrg_mixer_asset_create_mixer()=).
- A ramped send that reaches 0 stays in the graph (silent); =lrg_audio_mixer_set_send()= with 0 removes it.
- Ducking reads the trigger after its fader: a muted or solo-silenced trigger does not duck.
- Pitch above 1 shortens a one-shot voice; a looping voice wraps with interpolation across the loop point.
