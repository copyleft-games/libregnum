=lrg_audio_analyze (samples, n_samples, channels, rate)= or =lrg_audio_analyze_wave (wave)= measures a buffer and returns an =LrgAudioAnalysis=. Song renders use it to normalise. The editor and the IPC (=song.render= with =analyze=) report it, so a person or an AI agent can judge a mix by its numbers.

| Field | Unit | Meaning |
|-------+------+---------|
| =peak_db= | dBFS | The largest absolute sample |
| =true_peak_db= | dBTP | The largest peak between samples: 4x oversampled with a 48-tap windowed-sinc filter (BS.1770-4 Annex 2). What a DAC or a lossy encoder will produce. |
| =rms_db= | dBFS | Root mean square over every channel |
| =dc_offset= | linear | The mean sample value, the largest of the channels |
| =integrated_lufs= | LUFS | Gated programme loudness |
| =momentary_max_lufs= | LUFS | The loudest 400 ms window |
| =short_term_max_lufs= | LUFS | The loudest 3 s window |
| =loudness_range= | LU | EBU R128 loudness range (LRA) |
| =clipped_samples= | | Samples at or past full scale |

** How loudness is measured
1. Each channel goes through the K-weighting filter: a high shelf of about +4 dB above 1.5 kHz (the head's effect), then a 38 Hz high-pass. The coefficients are derived for any sample rate, the way libebur128 does.
2. Mean squares are taken over 100 ms segments and summed over the channels. Every channel weighs 1, except in 6-channel (5.1) input, where the LFE is left out and the surrounds weigh 1.41.
3. Momentary loudness uses 400 ms blocks (four segments, 75 % overlap). The integrated loudness averages the blocks louder than -70 LUFS (the absolute gate) and louder than 10 LU under the mean of those (the relative gate).
4. Short-term loudness uses 3 s windows every 100 ms. The loudness range is the spread from the 10th to the 95th percentile of the windows above -70 LUFS and above 20 LU under their mean (EBU Tech 3342).
5. Loudness = -0.691 + 10 log10 (mean square).

A stereo 1 kHz sine at -20 dBFS measures -20.0 LUFS, which is the EBU calibration and a test case. Clips shorter than 400 ms (integrated) or 3 s (short-term, range) report =LRG_AUDIO_LOUDNESS_SILENCE= (-144) for those fields.

** Normalisation
=lrg_audio_analysis_normalize_gain (analysis, loudness, target, ceiling)= returns the linear gain that brings the audio to =target=: the integrated loudness in LUFS when =loudness= is set, otherwise the sample peak in dBFS. For loudness, the gain is lowered if the true peak would go above =ceiling= dBTP. Silence gets 1. Common targets: -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP for streaming services, -16 LUFS for podcasts, -23 LUFS for broadcast (EBU R128).

** Tests
=tests/test-audio-encoder.c=: the -20 dBFS sine reading -20.0 LUFS, peak, RMS, range, the normalisation gain with and without a ceiling, silence, bad layouts, and a true peak 3 dB above the sample peak (a sine at a quarter of the rate sampled 45 degrees off its crests).
