#+title: LrgWorld3DRenderer
#+description: The shared 3D renderer: draws an LrgWorld3D (or any LrgRenderList) with PBR materials, lights, shadows, environment (sky, IBL, reflection probes, fog), SSAO, decals, LOD fades, real post-processing and debug views; the editor viewport draws through it too.

* LrgWorld3DRenderer
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lrgworld3drenderer
:END:

** Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: overview
:END:
=LrgWorld3DRenderer= (=src/world3d/lrg-world3d-renderer.{h,c}=, since 0.9.0) is the engine's one 3D drawing implementation. It takes an =LrgRenderList= (collected from an =LrgWorld3D= through a camera, or built by hand) and a view, and draws it with the engine's lighting: an =LrgLightSet= synced from the list's lights (directional, point and spot, with layers and shadow settings), the cascaded, cube and spot shadow maps of =LrgShadowRenderer=, the =LrgMaterial= of each item on the GGX metallic-roughness lit shader, the environment in effect (=LrgEnvironment=: sky, image-based light, reflection probes, distance and volumetric fog), a depth prepass with SSAO, projected decals, LOD cross-fades, opaque, cutout and transparent passes, optional static batching through =LrgStaticBatch=, the debug draw, and real post-processing through =lrg_post_processor_process()= on a half-float scene image (a =.rpost= stack or a processor). The frame, pass by pass with measured costs, is the subject of [[file:../../guides/rendering-pipeline.org][the rendering pipeline guide]].

It was extracted from the editor viewport (=LrgEditorSceneRenderer=, the most complete 3D renderer the engine had), and the editor now draws through it: authoring nodes become a transient render list, and while a 3D level plays the viewport draws the play session's =LrgWorld3D=. A game and the editor therefore draw the same scene the same way; the viewport's render modes (lit, unlit, wireframe, normals, lighting only, and the 0.9.0 debug views overdraw, UV, light complexity, AO and reflections) are the renderer's.

Without a window, or with =headless= set, a render runs every pass without touching the GPU and records what it would draw (per-pass counts and the entities in draw order), which is how its logic is tested.

#+begin_example
 LrgWorld3D --collect_render(camera)--> LrgRenderList --render_list(view)--> target
                                          │ items: OPAQUE / CUTOUT / TRANSPARENT / SHADOW_CASTER
                                          │ lights: LrgLight3DDesc
                                          ▼
   lights -> LrgLightSet   environment (bake if dirty) -> ambient, fog, IBL   decals -> select
   shadow passes (cascades, cube faces, spot maps): replay SHADOW_CASTER
   depth prepass (OPAQUE + CUTOUT) -> SSAO            (when SSAO or volumetric fog runs)
   clear (RGBA16F scene image when post runs)  ->  sky (environment or dome)
   -> "draw-background" -> OPAQUE (static batch first) -> CUTOUT -> volumetric fog
   -> TRANSPARENT -> "draw-overlay" -> debug draw -> labels -> post (process + copy)
#+end_example

** Concepts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts
:END:

*** Render lists and items
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-render-list
:END:
The renderer draws the contract defined in [[file:world3d.org::#render-list-contract][LrgRenderList]]: each =LrgRenderItem= is one draw (a primitive, a =GrlMesh=, a =GrlModel=, a billboard or a custom callback) with a world matrix, colour, optional texture and material, render layer and flags. =lrg_world3d_collect_render()= fills a list from every active, visible entity's components, keeps the items the camera's cull mask and frustum accept, adds the registered lights and sorts it: opaque and cutout by sort key then front to back, transparent back to front, shadow casters by sort key. The renderer replays each category in that order; it does not re-sort.

How each kind is drawn:

| Kind | Drawing |
|------+---------|
| =PRIMITIVE= | A unit mesh scaled by the item's =params= (0 means unit size): a 1x1x1 cube (=params= width, height, depth), spheres and discs of radius 0.5 (=params[0]= radius), cylinders and cones of radius 0.5 and height 1 standing centred on the origin (=params[0]= radius, =params[1]= height), a 1x1 plane in XZ (=params[0]= width, =params[1]= depth), a torus of major radius 0.375 (=params[0]= major radius, uniform scale), =RECTANGLE_2D= / =CIRCLE_2D= stood up into the XY plane, =GRID= as 10 lines of 0.1 (colour passes only), anything else as a wire box. |
| =MESH= | The =GrlMesh= with the item's colour and texture. |
| =MODEL= | Every mesh of the =GrlModel= (or only =mesh_index=) with its own material map; its diffuse colour is multiplied by the item colour and its texture replaced by the item texture when one is set. A mesh renderer component that left =model= =NULL= has its =mesh-asset= resolved through the renderer's cache; a model that cannot be loaded draws a magenta wire box. For an =LrgSkinnedModelComponent= item, =lrg_skinned_model_component_apply_pose()= runs right before the draw, so components sharing one model each draw their own pose. |
| =BILLBOARD= | A camera-facing quad at the item's position, =params[0]= wide and =params[1]= high (default 1 and twice the half width), unlit; =params[2]= is an =LrgBillboardMode=: =LRG_BILLBOARD_MODE_SPHERICAL= faces the camera fully, =LRG_BILLBOARD_MODE_AXIS_Y= stays upright and turns about world Y. An item of an =LrgBillboardComponent= without a texture has the component's texture path resolved through the renderer's cache (this also applies to its fixed-orientation plane items). Not a shadow caster. |
| =CUSTOM= | =draw_func (item, category, renderer)= inside the 3D pass with the pass's shader bound; =pass_data= is the =LrgWorld3DRenderer=. Called for =LRG_RENDER_CATEGORY_SHADOW_CASTER= in shadow passes. The renderer does not push the item's world matrix: the callback applies it (the tilemap component does). |

*Materials.* An item's =material= is an =LrgMaterial= (=.rmat=) or a legacy =LrgMaterial3D=. The item colour is used as it is: components fold their material's colour into it (a mesh renderer copies its =LrgMaterial='s base colour; its own colour also feeds its scalar =LrgMaterial3D=), so the renderer no longer multiplies the material colour in (before 0.9.0 that squared it). In the lit modes an =LrgMaterial= is bound per draw with =lrg_material_apply()= (maps on texture units 0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, =pbrParams=, =mapFlags=, =emissionOwn=, the alpha cutoff) and =lrg_material_apply_state()= (blend, culling, depth), its maps are given to raylib's =DrawMesh()= with =lrg_material_fill_raylib_maps()=, and afterwards =lrg_material_reset_uniforms()= / =lrg_material_restore_state()= put the "no material" state back; its unlit shading model draws with the lit shader's unlit path (albedo plus emission), and a =custom= model draws with its =.rshader= (compiled once per path, lit assets bound to the light set with =lrg_shader_asset_bind_lights()=). An =LrgMaterial3D= sets only =pbrParams= (its metallic and roughness) and =emissionOwn=. Model items keep their own per-mesh maps for the slots the material leaves empty. Items with an =LrgMaterial= are not static-batched. The shadow and prepass passes use the material's albedo map for the alpha test.

*LOD fade.* The lit, shadow-caster and depth-prepass shaders all start with =lrg_lod_fade_discard()= (the =lodFade= uniform of =lrg_lod_get_glsl()=). Before each draw the renderer sets =lodFade= to =lrg_lod_component_get_item_fade()= of the item (the dithered cross-fade of an =LrgLodComponent= between two levels) and back to 0 after a fading item, so programs other items use stay at 0.

*Layers.* The light set learns each item's render layer (=lrg_light_set_set_draw_layers()=, uploaded only when it changes) so a light's =layer_mask= lights only the layers it names.

Flags: =UNLIT= items use the unlit shader in the lit modes, =DOUBLE_SIDED= turns back-face culling off for the draw, =TRANSPARENT= and =CUTOUT= pick the category (see below), =CAST_SHADOW= puts the item into the shadow-caster list.

*** Passes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-passes
:END:
=lrg_world3d_renderer_render_list()= runs, in order (=LrgWorld3DRenderPass= names the recorded ones):

1. *Lights*: the list's lights go into the light set (=lrg_world3d_renderer_sync_lights()=).
2. *Environment*: the [[#concepts-look][look in effect]] is resolved; a dirty environment is baked (=lrg_environment_bake()=: radiance, irradiance, prefiltered specular, BRDF table; the caller's target is left and re-entered), its ambient and fog go onto the light set (=lrg_environment_apply_to_light_set()=) and, with IBL on, it is bound as the lit shader's environment hook (=lrg_light_set_set_environment()=). Without an environment the renderer's ambient stays and any fog an earlier environment set is cleared.
3. *Decals*: world renders refill the world's decal set from its =LrgDecalComponent=s (=lrg_decal_component_sync_world()=, textures resolved through the renderer's cache) and bind =lrg_decal_component_get_world_set()=; =render_list()= binds the renderer's own set (=lrg_world3d_renderer_get_decal_set()=). The set's origin follows the light set's, =lrg_decal_set_select()= picks the decals around the eye, and the set is bound with =lrg_light_set_set_decals()= when any were selected (and decals are on, and the mode is not lighting only).
4. *Shadows* (=SHADOW=): with =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_SHADOWS=, a lit mode and a perspective view, =lrg_shadow_renderer_update()= plans the passes around the view (3 cascades of the sun by default, 6 cube faces per shadowed point light and one perspective map per shadowed spot light) and the =SHADOW_CASTER= list is replayed once per pass between =lrg_shadow_renderer_begin_pass()= and =lrg_shadow_renderer_end_pass()= with the caster shader. Items replay when their layer is in both the view's cull mask and the shadow renderer's caster mask (=lrg_shadow_renderer_set_caster_mask()=). Billboards, grids and wire boxes cast nothing. When every pass rendered, the light set samples the shadow maps.
5. *Depth prepass* (=PREPASS=) and *SSAO* (=SSAO=): when SSAO is on (lit modes, perspective), in the AO view or the volumetric fog runs, the opaque and cutout items are drawn into an =LrgDepthPrepass= of the view's size (depth and view normals, alpha cutoff 0.5) through the view's own matrices; with SSAO on =lrg_ssao_render()= follows and the result is bound with =lrg_light_set_set_ssao()= (it multiplies the indirect light and the material occlusion).
6. *Clear*: to =background= (=LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_CLEAR=; black in the debug views), into the post processor's scene image when post-processing runs.
7. *Sky* (=SKY=): with =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_SKY= in a perspective view (not the debug views): the environment's sky (=lrg_environment_draw_sky()=) when one is in effect, else the sky dome, its sun opposite the light set's sun, radius half the far plane (1 .. 500).
8. *=draw-background=* signal (GPU renders): extra background geometry with the view loaded (the editor's grid).
9. *Opaque* (=OPAQUE=): the static batch first (=LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_BATCH_STATIC=, lit mode), then every other opaque item. Per item: the material, the LOD fade, the draw layer and, when the bound environment has reflection probes, the [[#concepts-probes][probe pick]].
10. *Cutout* (=CUTOUT=): the light set's alpha cutoff is 0.5 for this pass (fragments below it are discarded), then back to 0; a material's own cutoff wins for its draw.
11. *Volumetric fog* (=VOLUMETRIC=): when the environment enables it (a volumetric density above 0) and =volumetric-enabled= is on (lit modes, perspective), =lrg_volumetric_fog_render()= marches the prepass depth with the sun and its cascades and composites the in-scattered light over the opaque scene.
12. *Transparent* (=TRANSPARENT=): back to front with depth writes off, blended with raylib's alpha blending (or the material's blend mode).
13. *=draw-overlay=* signal (GPU renders), still in wire mode for the wireframe mode: editor icons and helpers.
14. *Debug draw*: =lrg_debug_draw_draw_3d()= with =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_DEBUG_DRAW= and a debug draw set; after the 3D mode ends, =lrg_debug_draw_draw_labels()=.
15. *Post* (=POST=): with =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_POST= and a post processor with effects (not in the debug views), the colour passes drew into a half-float scene image (RGBA16F, RGBA8 when the driver cannot render to it) of the view's size; =lrg_post_processor_process()= runs every enabled effect over it in priority order and the result is copied into the original target. The pass count is the number of effect passes that ran.

The view and projection matrices are loaded straight into rlgl (the same =lrg_math3d_look_at()= / =lrg_math3d_perspective()= the editor's picking uses), so the image and picking agree pixel for pixel. The target is left bound with its 2D projection, ready for 2D overlays.

*** Render modes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-render-modes
:END:
| Mode | Shader | Notes |
|------+--------+-------|
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_LIT= | The light set's shader (sun, clustered point lights, ambient, fog, shadows); the fallback lit shader (one two-sided sun plus ambient 0.35) when the light set has none | Shadows and static batching only here |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_UNLIT= | Flat albedo (texture times colour) | No shadows |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_WIREFRAME= | Unlit, rlgl wire mode | No shadows |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_NORMALS= | World normals as colours (x red, y green, z blue) | No shadows |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_LIGHTING_ONLY= | The lit shader on white surfaces | Shadows, SSAO, no batching, no decals |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_OVERDRAW= | Every fragment adds a dim orange (0.10, 0.045, 0.015) with additive blending and no depth test | A heat map of shading cost: each layer covering a pixel brightens it |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_UV= | U in red and V in green (wrapped to 0 .. 1) over an 8 x 8 checker | Texel density, stretching and seams |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_LIGHT_COMPLEXITY= | The lit library walking the grid cell (or the active list) and counting the point and spot lights whose range reaches the fragment | Ramp: near black 0, blue 1, green 2-3, yellow 4-7, red 8 or more |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_AO= | The material occlusion times =lrg_ssao()= on white | Runs the prepass and SSAO even while SSAO is off, with =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_ssao()='s parameters |
| =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_MODE_REFLECTIONS= | =lrg_env_specular()= at each surface's roughness (environment blended with the picked probes), no Fresnel | Black without an environment |

The values equal =LrgEditorRenderMode= one to one. The five debug views (overdraw to reflections) clear to black and skip the sky and the post chain so their colours read as they are; light complexity, AO and reflections are programs built from =lrg_light_set_get_glsl_library()= and bound with =lrg_light_set_bind_shader()=, so they see exactly the lights, SSAO and environment the lit pass sees. The scene GLSL is built in (=lrg_world3d_renderer_get_shader_source()=: =scene.vs=, =lit.fs=, =unlit.fs=, =normals.fs=); the readable copies are =data/editor/shaders/*.glsl=. The 0.8 one-pass post shader (=post.fs=) is gone.

*** Lights
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-lights
:END:
=lrg_world3d_renderer_sync_lights()= maps =LrgLight3DDesc= records onto the light set:

- the first enabled *directional* light is the sun: direction normalised, colour times intensity clamped to 8 bits; further directional lights are ignored;
- every enabled *point* and *spot* light goes through =lrg_light_set_add_light_desc()=: position, range as radius, colour times intensity, the spot's inner and outer cone, its =layer_mask= (applied per surface through the draw layers), shadow priority 1 when =cast_shadows= is set (cube maps for points, a perspective map for spots) and, from an =LrgLightComponent= source, its shadow bias;
- disabled lights are skipped;
- without a directional light the *fallback sun* (direction 0.5, -0.75, -0.45; colour 214, 206, 192) lights the scene while =fallback-sun= is on (the default); with it off the sun is black.

Ambient is 58, 62, 78 unless =lrg_world3d_renderer_set_ambient()= changes it.

*** The look: environment and post stack
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-look
:END:
The environment (sky, IBL, probes, fog, volumetric fog) and the post stack a render uses come from three layers, highest first:

| Layer | Set with | Used by |
|-------+----------+---------|
| The renderer's own (override) | =lrg_world3d_renderer_set_environment()=, =lrg_world3d_renderer_set_post_stack()= | Callers that want to force a look: the editor's =.rpost= editor previews its stack here |
| The rendered world's | =lrg_world3d_set_environment()=, =lrg_world3d_set_post_stack()= (=lrg_level_instantiate_3d()= sets the level's) | =render()= and =render_view()= only (=render_list()= has no world): games and play-in-editor |
| The fallback | =lrg_world3d_renderer_set_fallback_look()= | The editor viewport feeds the edited level's =environment:= block and its =post_stack:= here while authoring |

=lrg_world3d_renderer_get_active_environment()= tells which environment the last render used. A post stack in effect is built into an internal =LrgPostProcessor= every frame with =lrg_post_stack_build()= (cheap when the effects match) and replaces =post-processor=; it runs only with =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_POST=. Without any layer the renderer draws the sky dome and the flat ambient, exactly as before 0.9.0.

Environment maps bake lazily before the first frame that needs them (and again when =lrg_environment_needs_bake()= says a setting changed); =lrg_world3d_renderer_bake_environment()= moves that cost to a loading screen. =ibl-enabled= (=lrg_world3d_renderer_set_ibl_enabled()=) switches the image-based light off while keeping the sky; the environment's own IBL flag does the same per environment.

*** Reflection probes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-probes
:END:
Probes live on the environment (=lrg_environment_add_probe()=; an =LrgReflectionProbeComponent= puts its probe on its world's environment). =lrg_world3d_renderer_capture_probes()= captures every probe of the environment in effect, and =lrg_world3d_renderer_capture_probe()= one probe from any list: the six faces show the environment's sky and the lit opaque and cutout items (no shadows, SSAO, transparency or post), then the probe prefilters them.

Probes are picked *per item*: before each lit draw the renderer asks =lrg_environment_select_probes()= for the two ready probes with the strongest influence at the item's centre (its bounds, else its origin) and, when that pair changes, binds the environment again with that reference point. An object inside a probe box therefore reflects that probe from any camera position, and objects outside every box reflect the environment's sky. Within the bound pair the shader blends per fragment (=lrg_env_set_point()=), so the blend distance fades across box edges.

*** Assets
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-assets
:END:
The renderer caches =GrlModel=, =GrlTexture= and =LrgMaterial= (=.rmat=, =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_material()=, loadable without a window) objects by absolute path; failures are cached too (and logged once). An asset string is resolved by the resolver (=lrg_world3d_renderer_set_asset_resolver()=, for GUIDs or virtual paths) when it returns a path, otherwise absolute paths stay as they are and relative ones resolve against =base-dir= (the working directory without one). A per-string lookup table makes repeated lookups one hash probe; it is dropped when the base directory or resolver changes. =lrg_world3d_renderer_forget_asset()= drops one file (live reload), =lrg_world3d_renderer_reload_asset()= reloads it at once, and =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_generation()= counts these changes for views that render on demand. Loading needs a window: without one =get_model()= / =get_texture()= return =NULL= and cache nothing.

*** Static batching
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-static-batching
:END:
With =LRG_WORLD3D_RENDER_FLAG_BATCH_STATIC= in the lit mode, the opaque items of entities marked static (=lrg_entity3d_set_static()=) that are solid primitives or =GrlMesh= meshes, untextured, single-sided and lit, are merged into one =LrgStaticBatch= (colours baked into vertex colours, one chunk per 65535 vertices and layer) and drawn in a few calls with the light-set shader. The batch is rebuilt only when the set changes (entity, world version, shape, parameters, colour, matrix or layer); everything else draws item by item. Shadow passes replay the items individually. =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_batched_count()= reports how many items went through the batch.

*** Headless recording
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-headless
:END:
Without a window, or with =headless= set, a render plans the shadow passes (=lrg_shadow_renderer_update()= is headless), replays every category and records each draw: =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_pass_count()= per pass, =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_recorded_entity()= in draw order, =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_shadow_pass_count()=, =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_batched_count()=. The hooks (=draw-background=, =draw-overlay=) are not emitted and nothing is cleared. The same records are kept on GPU renders.

** Format
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: format
:END:
The renderer has no file format of its own: it draws render lists. What it draws comes from =.rlevel= nodes (through =lrg_level_instantiate_3d()= and the world3d components), the level's =environment:= block (see [[file:../lighting/environment.org][LrgEnvironment]]) with its =post_stack:= (a [[file:../postprocess/post-stack.org][.rpost]] file), materials ([[file:../graphics/material.org][.rmat]]) and asset files (models: glTF/GLB, OBJ, IQM, VOX, M3D; images: anything raylib loads plus =.pep=).

*** Rendering
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: api-rendering
:END:
| Function | Description |
|----------+-------------|

*** A game loop
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-game-loop
:END:
#+begin_src c
g_autoptr(LrgWorld3D)         world = lrg_world3d_new ();
g_autoptr(LrgWorld3DRenderer) renderer = lrg_world3d_renderer_new ();
g_autoptr(LrgCamera3D)        camera = lrg_camera3d_new ();
g_autoptr(LrgEntity3D)        crate = lrg_entity3d_new_with_name ("Crate");
g_autoptr(LrgEntity3D)        sun = lrg_entity3d_new_with_name ("Sun");
g_autoptr(LrgMeshRendererComponent) mesh = lrg_mesh_renderer_component_new_primitive (LRG_PRIMITIVE_CUBE);
g_autoptr(LrgLightComponent)  light = lrg_light_component_new_with_kind (LRG_LIGHT3D_DIRECTIONAL);
const gfloat                  tilt[3] = { -1.0f, 0.6f, 0.0f };

InitWindow (1280, 720, "game");
lrg_entity3d_set_position_xyz (crate, 0.0f, 0.5f, 0.0f);
lrg_entity3d_add_component (crate, LRG_COMPONENT (mesh));
lrg_world3d_add_entity (world, crate);
lrg_entity3d_set_rotation_euler (sun, tilt);          /* lights shine down local -Z */
lrg_entity3d_add_component (sun, LRG_COMPONENT (light));
lrg_world3d_add_entity (world, sun);

lrg_camera3d_set_position_xyz (camera, 6.0f, 4.0f, 6.0f);
lrg_camera3d_set_target_xyz (camera, 0.0f, 0.5f, 0.0f);
lrg_world3d_register_camera (world, LRG_CAMERA (camera), 0);
lrg_world3d_renderer_set_base_dir (renderer, "assets");

while (!WindowShouldClose ())
{
	lrg_world3d_step (world, GetFrameTime ());     /* fixed, update, late (lights re-register) */
	BeginDrawing ();
	lrg_world3d_renderer_render (renderer, world, NULL, NULL, 0, 0);   /* active camera, screen */
	DrawFPS (10, 10);
	EndDrawing ();
}
lrg_world3d_renderer_release (renderer);              /* before the context goes */
CloseWindow ();
#+end_src

** Performance notes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: performance
:END:
- One render costs: a light-set rebuild (O(lights)), one shadow replay of the caster list per shadow pass (3 cascades by default: each caster is drawn 3 more times; point-light shadows add 6 per light), and one draw per item and model mesh in the colour passes. Keep =CAST_SHADOW= off for small props, or put them on a layer the caster mask leaves out.
- Static scenery: mark entities static and turn =BATCH_STATIC= on; thousands of props become a handful of draws. The merge is CPU work done only when the static set changes; moving a static entity (its world version changes) re-merges everything, so do not animate static entities.
- Model and texture lookups are one hash probe per item after the first; =lrg_world3d_collect_render()= reuses the list's arrays when you reuse the list (the renderer does).
- The cutout pass re-applies the light-set uniforms twice (on, then off), only when cutout items exist.
- An =LrgMaterial= costs a uniform upload, its texture binds and a render-state flush per draw (plus a reset after it); an =LrgMaterial3D= two uniform uploads. Items with an =LrgMaterial= are not static-batched.
- The depth prepass draws the opaque and cutout items once more; SSAO and the volumetric fog are full-screen passes (half resolution options on both). The environment bake and probe captures are one-off costs (hundreds of milliseconds on software GL): bake on a loading screen with =lrg_world3d_renderer_bake_environment()=.
- Probe picks are one =lrg_environment_select_probes()= per item; the environment is rebound only when the picked pair changes, so sort-by-position lists rebind rarely.
- Post-processing renders the scene into a full-size half-float image plus one pass per enabled effect; images are kept per processor and resized only when the target size changes.
- Measured per-pass costs (software GL) are in [[file:../../guides/rendering-pipeline.org::#costs][the rendering pipeline guide]].
- Headless recording costs about the same CPU as a GPU render minus the draw calls; it allocates nothing per frame after the first.

** Pitfalls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pitfalls
:END:
- *Release before closing the window.* =lrg_world3d_renderer_release()= unloads meshes, shaders, cached models and textures, shadow atlases and post images. After =CloseWindow()= only bookkeeping can be dropped; cached =GrlModel= / =GrlTexture= objects are then leaked on purpose (their finalizers would call GL).
- *Do not keep the light set across a release*: it is replaced (its shader belongs to the old context).
- *Shadows, SSAO and volumetric fog need a lit mode and a perspective view*; orthographic views and the unlit / wireframe / normals / debug modes skip them (the debug views also skip the sky and post).
- *The world's look only applies to world renders*: =render_list()= has no world, so give it an environment with =set_environment()= or =set_fallback_look()=.
- *Probes need a capture*: a probe that never captured is not bound. Capture after loading (=capture_probes()=) and again when the scene around a probe changes.
- *The scene image is half-float*: effects receive values above 1 (bloom and the tonemap want them); put the tonemap before effects that expect display values.
- *The target must be bound for =render_view()= / =render_list()=*; =render()= binds a =GrlRenderTexture= itself. The shadow and post passes switch framebuffers and come back to the bound one; do not call them inside =BeginMode3D()=.
- *Sorting is the list's*: =render_list()= replays the categories in their stored order. Call =lrg_render_list_sort()= after =lrg_render_list_set_view()= for a hand-built list.
- *Debug draw frames*: the renderer draws the debug batch but does not call =lrg_debug_draw_end_frame()= (several views may share one batch); end the frame once per frame yourself or one-frame primitives pile up.
- *Model colours multiply*: a model's material colour is multiplied by the item colour (white by default), never replaced.
- *Without a window* =get_model()= / =get_texture()= return =NULL= and cache nothing, so a missing asset is only "missing" (=is_asset_missing()=) by file existence.

** Using it from the editor
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: editor
:END:
The editor viewport draws through this renderer (see [[file:../../editor/editor-app.org::#scene-renderer][Scene renderer]]): =LrgEditorSceneRenderer= owns one (=lrg_editor_scene_renderer_get_runtime_renderer()=), turns the level's nodes into a render list every frame (primitives, models, sprites as textured quads, CAD parts, LIGHT nodes as =LrgLight3DDesc= lights with their spot cones and =cast_shadows=, =material:= references as =LrgMaterial=s from =lrg_world3d_renderer_get_material()=, mesh assets whose =.meta= asks for LOD levels at the level the viewport camera selects or the inspector forces (=lrg_editor_scene_renderer_get_lod_group()=), decal nodes as decals of the renderer's set, reflection-probe nodes as probes captured when they move or on =lrg_editor_scene_renderer_recapture_probes()=), feeds the level's =environment:= block and =post_stack:= as the [[#concepts-look][fallback look]], applies each viewport's render-layer mask (=lrg_editor_scene_renderer_set_cull_mask()=), maps the viewport's render mode and overlays onto =render-mode= and =flags= (=SHADOWS= / =SKY= in perspective views, =POST= with the viewport's own processor), and draws the grid, icons, selection bounds, tilemap grids and missing-asset placeholders from the =draw-background= and =draw-overlay= hooks. While a 3D level plays, the viewport draws the play session's =LrgWorld3D= with =lrg_world3d_renderer_render_view()= and the world's own lights, so play-in-editor shows exactly what the game draws. The view's =Render Mode= menu, the =view.render_mode= / =view.overlay= IPC methods and =lrgeditorctl= =view render-mode= / =view overlay= therefore drive this renderer.
