* LrgFracture - Voronoi Pre-Fracture
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: fracture
:END:
=LrgFracture= (=src/physics/lrg-fracture.h=, GIR =Libregnum.Fracture=) breaks a closed convex mesh (a wall, a crate, a pillar, a rock) into convex chunks ahead of time, so that at run time an object can be swapped for its pieces in one call. The pieces are the cells of a 3D Voronoi diagram of a set of seed points, clipped to the mesh. They keep the original surface (its material ids and texture coordinates) and gain flat *interior* faces where they were cut, tagged with an interior material id so a renderer can give the inside its own look (raw concrete, splintered wood).

Everything but =lrg_fracture_chunk_create_mesh()= is headless. Results are deterministic (seeds come from a seeded =GRand=, clipping runs in doubles in a fixed order) and can be cached in a =.rfracture= file. In a 3D world use [[file:../world3d/components.org::#destructible-component][LrgDestructibleComponent]], which derives the fracture from the entity's cube, listens for hard hits and does the swap.

** Concepts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts
:END:
*** Voronoi cells
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-voronoi
:END:
The cell of seed /i/ is every point closer to /i/ than to any other seed: the intersection of the half-spaces behind the bisector planes of (/i/, /j/) for all /j/. Intersected with a convex mesh it is a convex polyhedron, so every chunk is convex (and a cheap convex-hull collision shape).

The builder starts each cell as the source mesh's faces (as polygons) and clips them by the bisector planes, nearest seed first: each face is cut with Sutherland-Hodgman, the points created on the plane are deduplicated and sorted by angle round their centroid into one convex *cap* face (the new interior face), and points left collinear on any face are dropped. A seed further away than twice the cell's current radius cannot cut it, so the loop stops early: building is roughly O(seeds x neighbours) rather than O(seeds²).

Every cap records the seed that cut it: chunks that share a cap are *neighbours* (=lrg_fracture_chunk_get_neighbors()=), the connectivity a progressive or structural destruction model walks.

*** Source mesh
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-source
:END:
=lrg_fracture_set_mesh()= takes a closed (every directed edge has exactly one opposite), counter-clockwise (outward) wound, convex triangle mesh with optional per-vertex texture coordinates and per-triangle material ids. Non-convex meshes are refused with =LRG_FRACTURE_ERROR_NOT_CONVEX=: split them into convex parts (each a destructible of its own) or give a convex proxy. =lrg_fracture_set_box()= makes the common case (walls, crates, slabs) a one-liner with per-face 0..1 texture coordinates.

*** Seeds
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-seeds
:END:
| Mode (=LrgFractureSeedMode=) | Placement |
|------------------------------+-----------|
| =LRG_FRACTURE_SEED_RANDOM= | =chunk-count= uniform points inside the mesh (rejection sampling in the bounds) |
| =LRG_FRACTURE_SEED_IMPACT= | points round the impact point (=lrg_fracture_set_impact_point()=), radius r u³ for uniform u, r = =impact-radius= (0: half the bounds diagonal): small shards at the hit, large pieces far away |
| =LRG_FRACTURE_SEED_GRID= | a grid whose cell count is closest to =chunk-count= with cells as cubic as possible, each seed jittered by =grid-jitter= of a cell (0: regular bricks) |
| =LRG_FRACTURE_SEED_CUSTOM= | the points of =lrg_fracture_set_seed_points()= |

Seeds outside the mesh, and duplicates, make no chunk.

*** Chunks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-chunks
:END:
An =LrgFractureChunk= is an immutable, reference-counted boxed record in the *source frame* (not centred): render geometry with one corner per face vertex (flat normals), triangles wound outward, a material id per triangle, its distinct corner points (its hull), neighbours, and exact mass properties computed from the polyhedron with Eberly's polyhedral integrals: volume, centre of mass and the inertia tensor about it (=lrg_fracture_chunk_get_mass_properties()= follows the =lrg_physics_shape_get_mass_properties()= contract). The chunk volumes sum to the source volume up to float rounding.

*** Swap-in
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts-swap
:END:
A chunk's body has its origin at the chunk's centre of mass: =lrg_fracture_chunk_create_shape()= is a convex hull of the chunk relative to it, =lrg_fracture_get_chunk_transform()= places it under the intact object's (rigid) world matrix, and =lrg_fracture_chunk_create_mesh (chunk, TRUE, ...)= is the matching render mesh. =lrg_fracture_swap_in()= does all of it: one dynamic body per chunk in an =LrgDynamicsWorld= with mass =density= x volume, in the given object layer (usually =LRG_PHYSICS_LAYER_DEBRIS=), launched by =lrg_fracture_get_chunk_velocity()=. Removing the intact object's body is the caller's job (the component does it).

The break-up velocity model: every chunk gets the impulse divided by the object's total mass, weighted by =2 r / (r + d)= (d: the chunk centre's distance to the impact, r: the object's bounding radius), plus an outward burst of 10 % of that speed away from the impact. Chunks at the hit fly up to twice the mean speed, the far side barely moves; the total momentum is of the order of the impulse (not exactly it).

=lrg_fracture_should_break()= compares an impulse magnitude with =impulse-threshold=.

** The .rfracture format
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: format
:END:
A YAML document (flow sequences for the arrays). Floats are written with nine significant digits, enough for an exact float round trip. Unknown keys are ignored; =format= and =version= are required, the parameters keep the loading object's values when missing, =chunks= is required.

#+begin_src yaml
# libregnum pre-fractured mesh (Voronoi chunks)
format: rfracture            # required
version: 1                   # LRG_FRACTURE_FORMAT_VERSION; others are refused
source_hash: "9f2c...e1"     # SHA-256 of the source mesh and every build parameter
                             # (lrg_fracture_get_source_hash); absent when saved without a source
seed_mode: random            # random | impact | grid | custom (LrgFractureSeedMode nick)
chunk_count: 12              # requested seeds
seed: 7                      # GRand seed
interior_material: 1         # material id of the cut faces
impact_radius: 0             # 0 = half the bounds diagonal
grid_jitter: 0.300000012
impulse_threshold: 50        # N s
density: 1000                # kg / m^3
impact_point: [0.5, 0.35, 0.150000006]   # only when set
source_volume: 0.400000006   # informational
source_bounds: [-1, -0.5, -0.100000001, 1, 0.5, 0.100000001]   # min xyz, max xyz
seeds: [0.12, -0.3, 0.02, ...]           # 3 floats per seed actually used
chunks:
  - seed: [0.12, -0.3, 0.02]             # the cell's seed
    volume: 0.0312
    center_of_mass: [0.1, -0.28, 0.0]    # source frame
    inertia: [ ...9 floats... ]          # unit density, about the COM, column-major
    bounds: [ ...6 floats... ]           # min xyz, max xyz
    neighbors: [3, 5, 8]                 # chunk indices sharing a cut face
    positions: [ ... ]                   # 3 floats per corner, source frame
    normals: [ ... ]                     # 3 floats per corner (flat)
    texcoords: [ ... ]                   # 2 floats per corner
    indices: [0, 1, 2, ...]              # triangles, outward winding
    materials: [0, 0, 1, ...]            # one id per triangle; interior_material = cut face
    hull: [ ... ]                        # distinct corner points (collision hull)
#+end_src

=lrg_fracture_load_or_build()= is the cache workflow: when the file exists and its =source_hash= equals the hash of the current source and parameters it is loaded, otherwise the fracture is built and the file rewritten. A game can also ship only the =.rfracture= (=lrg_fracture_new_from_file()=): chunks and parameters load without a source mesh.

** Worked examples
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: examples
:END:
*** Pre-fracture a wall and cache it
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-cache
:END:
#+begin_src c
g_autoptr(LrgFracture) wall = lrg_fracture_new ();
const gfloat           half[3] = { 2.0f, 1.25f, 0.15f };      /* 4 x 2.5 x 0.3 m */
g_autoptr(GError)      error = NULL;

lrg_fracture_set_box (wall, half, NULL);
g_object_set (wall, "chunk-count", 40, "seed-mode", LRG_FRACTURE_SEED_GRID,
              "grid-jitter", 0.5f, "density", 2400.0f, "impulse-threshold", 400.0f, NULL);
if (!lrg_fracture_load_or_build (wall, "levels/wall.rfracture", &error))
	g_warning ("fracture: %s", error->message);
#+end_src

*** Break it on a hard hit
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-break
:END:
#+begin_src c
static void
on_contact (LrgDynamicsWorld *world, LrgDynamicsContact *c, Wall *w)
{
	if ((c->body_a == w->body || c->body_b == w->body)
	    && lrg_fracture_should_break (w->fracture, c->normal_impulse))
	{
		w->hit_pending = TRUE;               /* swap outside the signal emission */
		memcpy (w->hit_point, c->point, sizeof w->hit_point);
		w->hit_impulse = c->normal_impulse;
	}
}

/* next fixed step */
if (w->hit_pending)
{
	gfloat            impulse[3] = { 0.0f, 0.0f, -w->hit_impulse };
	g_autoptr(GArray) ids = lrg_fracture_swap_in (w->fracture, world, w->matrix, w->hit_point,
	                                              impulse, LRG_PHYSICS_LAYER_DEBRIS, NULL);

	lrg_dynamics_world_remove_body (world, w->body, NULL);
	w->hit_pending = FALSE;
}
#+end_src

*** Draw the chunks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example-draw
:END:
#+begin_src c
for (i = 0; i < ids->len; i++)
{
	gfloat m[16];

	if (meshes[i] == NULL)    /* once, with GL */
		meshes[i] = lrg_fracture_chunk_create_mesh (lrg_fracture_get_chunk (wall, i), TRUE, NULL);
	lrg_dynamics_world_get_transform (world, g_array_index (ids, guint, i), m, NULL);
	draw_mesh_with_matrix (meshes[i], m);      /* the body origin is the chunk's COM */
}
#+end_src

** Performance
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: performance
:END:
=tests/test-fracture.c= (=/fracture/perf=, release build, one core): a 4 x 3 x 0.3 m wall into 64 random chunks builds in 5.7 .. 6.0 ms pinned with =taskset= (up to 9 ms unpinned on a loaded machine). A swap-in costs one convex hull build plus one body insertion per chunk (tens of microseconds each). =.rfracture= files take a few kilobytes per chunk; loading parses YAML (a few ms for 64 chunks). Build at load time (=lrg_destructible_component_prepare()=) or ship the cache; never build in the frame of the hit.

** Pitfalls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pitfalls
:END:
- *Convex only.* Non-convex sources are refused; fracture convex parts separately.
- *Welded, outward wound.* Positions must be shared between triangles (texture coordinates may differ only through =set_box='s internal handling); inward winding reads as non-convex.
- *Scale is baked.* Chunks live in the source frame; the swap's object matrix must be rigid. Bake the entity's scale into the source (the component does).
- *Impact seeds are small.* With few seeds most land near the hit; raise =chunk-count= or =impact-radius= for a more even break.
- *Do not swap inside a physics signal.* Queue the break and swap in the next fixed step (adding bodies while the world emits is not supported).
- *The hash covers the parameters,* not =impulse-threshold= or =density= (they do not change the geometry): changing those keeps the cache.
- *One material per chunk mesh.* =lrg_fracture_chunk_create_mesh()= is one mesh; use the per-triangle material ids to build submeshes when the interior needs its own material.

:PROPERTIES:
