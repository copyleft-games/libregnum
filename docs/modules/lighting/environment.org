* LrgEnvironment - Sky, Image-Based Lighting, Ambient and Fog
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lrgenvironment
:END:
#+begin_quote
*[[file:../../index.org][Home]]* > *[[file:../index.org][Modules]]* > *[[file:index.org][Lighting]]* > Environment
#+end_quote

=LrgEnvironment= describes the light of a 3D scene that does not come from a light source: the sky, the image-based lighting (IBL) derived from it, the flat ambient fallback, the distance fog, the volumetric fog parameters, the level's post-processing stack and the local [[file:reflection-probe.org][reflection probes]]. It is the object behind the =environment:= block of =.rlevel= files ([[file:../../editor/yaml-format.org::#environment][format]]) and plugs into the lit shader of [[file:../graphics/light-set.org][LrgLightSet]] through the =LRG_ENV_GLSL= hook (=lrg_light_set_set_environment()=).

- *Type* - =LrgEnvironment=, final GObject, =LRG_TYPE_ENVIRONMENT=, GIR =Libregnum.Environment=
- *Header* - =lighting/lrg-environment.h= (through =<libregnum.h>=)
- *Enums* - =LrgEnvironmentSkySource= (=LRG_TYPE_ENVIRONMENT_SKY_SOURCE=), =LrgEnvironmentError= (=LRG_TYPE_ENVIRONMENT_ERROR=, domain =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_ERROR=)
- *Signal* - =changed= (no arguments): any setting or probe changed; =lrg_environment_needs_bake()= tells whether the GPU maps are stale
- *Since* - 0.9.0

** Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-overview
:END:
Everything about an environment is plain data until it is baked. =lrg_environment_bake()= (needs a GL context) turns the sky into four GPU products:

| Product | Format | Used for |
|---------+--------+----------|
| radiance cubemap | RGBA16F, =resolution= edge, one level | the sky itself (=lrg_environment_draw_sky()=), the source of the other maps |
| irradiance | 27 floats (9 L2 spherical-harmonic coefficients x RGB), uniforms | diffuse light, =lrg_env_diffuse()= |
| prefiltered specular cubemap | RGBA16F, full mip chain down to 1 x 1 | glossy reflections, =lrg_env_specular()= |
| BRDF lookup table | 64 x 64 RGB32F (scale, bias) | split-sum Fresnel/visibility term, =lrg_env_brdf()= |

The light set's shader computes the indirect light of a surface as =kd * albedo * lrg_env_diffuse(n) + lrg_env_specular(n, v, rough) * (F0 * brdf.x + brdf.y)= when the environment is active, and as =ambientColor * albedo= otherwise.

** Concepts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-concepts
:END:
*** Sky sources
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-sky-sources
:END:
| Source | Data | How it is baked |
|--------+------+-----------------|
| =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_SKY_COLOR= | one sRGB colour | CPU faces |
| =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_SKY_GRADIENT= | zenith / horizon / ground colours | CPU faces; colour = mix (horizon, zenith or ground, sqrt (abs (y))) in linear space |
| =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_SKY_DOME= | an [[file:../graphics/sky-dome.org][LrgSkyDome]] | the dome is drawn into the six faces on the GPU, so its sun disc and horizon band show in reflections; irradiance is read back. The sky rotation is ignored (the dome has its own sun direction) |
| =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_SKY_CUBEMAP= | six face images or one cross | CPU faces sampled bilinearly from the images |
| =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_SKY_EQUIRECT= | an equirectangular [[file:../graphics/hdr-image.org][LrgHdrImage]] | CPU faces; a panorama wider than 4 x resolution is box-reduced first so the sun does not alias |

Colours given to the environment (sky colour, gradient, ambient, fog) are sRGB-encoded 0..1 like every other engine colour; they are decoded with gamma 2.2 (the light set's model) before any lighting maths. Images: =.hdr= files are linear radiance, other formats (PNG, JPG...) are decoded from sRGB. All derived maps hold linear radiance, and the hook functions return linear values.

Paths of images are kept as authored (=lrg_environment_set_sky_path()=, =lrg_environment_set_sky_face_path()=, a plain path or =guid:<uuid>=) and loaded by =lrg_environment_load_sources()=, which resolves =guid:= through an [[file:../core/asset-manager.org][LrgAssetManager]] and relative paths against a base directory (normally the level file's directory). Missing images at bake time fall back to the gradient with a warning.

*** Exposure and rotation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-exposure
:END:
The exposure is in stops: the sky, its diffuse and specular light and the flat ambient are multiplied by 2^exposure. The rotation turns the sky about +Y (degrees, counter-clockwise seen from above); a world direction =d= looks the sky up at =R_y(-rotation) d=. Both are uniforms: changing them needs no bake.

*** Irradiance: why L2 spherical harmonics
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-irradiance
:END:
Diffuse light at a normal =n= is the irradiance E(n) = integral over the sphere of L(w) max (0, n . w) dw. It is a very low-frequency function of =n=, and Ramamoorthi and Hanrahan ("An Efficient Representation for Irradiance Environment Maps", 2001) showed that its projection onto the first nine real spherical harmonics is accurate to about 3% on average for natural lighting. The engine uses that representation instead of an irradiance cubemap:

- *projection*: L_lm = sum over cube texels of L(w) Y_lm(w) dw, with the texel solid angle dw = (2/N)^2 / (1 + u^2 + v^2)^(3/2) (normalized so the total is exactly 4 pi);
- *convolution*: multiply band l by the clamped-cosine coefficients A_0 = pi, A_1 = 2 pi / 3, A_2 = pi / 4, and divide by pi so that evaluating gives E(n) / pi directly (the factor a Lambert surface needs);
- *evaluation*: =lrg_env_diffuse(n)= = sum c_i Y_i(n), clamped at 0, times exposure and diffuse intensity.

Consequences: no texture unit and no extra render passes for diffuse light; a constant sky gives exactly that colour (the test suite checks it); very sharp skies ring slightly (negative lobes are clamped). The coefficients are stored coefficient-major (=c0.rgb, c1.rgb, ... c8.rgb=), the basis order is Y00, Y1-1 (y), Y10 (z), Y11 (x), Y2-2 (xy), Y2-1 (yz), Y20 (3z^2 - 1), Y21 (xz), Y22 (x^2 - y^2) in world axes.

*** Specular: prefiltered mips and the split sum
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-specular
:END:
Glossy reflections use Karis' split-sum approximation ("Real Shading in Unreal Engine 4", 2013): the integral of radiance times the GGX/Smith BRDF is split into (a) the radiance convolved with the GGX lobe around the reflection vector (assuming n = v = r), stored per roughness in the mips of the prefiltered cubemap, and (b) the integral of the BRDF against white light, stored in the 2D BRDF table as F0 x scale + bias.

- Mip =m= of =M= levels holds roughness =m / (M - 1)= (perceptual roughness; alpha = roughness^2). Mip 0 is an exact copy of the radiance. The shader samples =textureLod (map, R_y(-rotation) r, roughness * (M - 1))=.
- Each texel of each mip is computed by GGX importance sampling (64 Hammersley samples) of the reflected direction. To avoid the noise of few samples on a high-resolution source, each sample reads a box-reduced copy of the radiance whose texel solid angle matches the sample's (filtered importance sampling: lod = 0.5 log2 (sample solid angle / texel solid angle) + 1, where the sample solid angle is 1 / (N_samples x pdf) and pdf = D / 4).
- The BRDF table is integrated on the CPU (256 samples per entry, Smith-Schlick G with the IBL remapping k = alpha / 2): x is n.v, y is roughness, texel centres at (i + 0.5) / 64. At roughness 0 it is exactly (1 - (1 - nv)^5, (1 - nv)^5).
- When the light set's hook window cannot spare a unit for the table (a 16-unit driver), the shader uses Karis' analytic fit of the table instead ("Physically Based Shading on Mobile", 2014).

*** GL 3.3 without compute
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-gl33
:END:
Every GPU step is a full-screen quad drawn into one face of one mip of a cubemap attached to a framebuffer (=rlFramebufferAttach()= with =RL_ATTACHMENT_CUBEMAP_POSITIVE_X + face= and a mip level). Face =f= and texture coordinates (s, t) map to a direction exactly as the OpenGL cubemap lookup does (=lrg_environment_cube_face_direction()=). Mip chains are never generated with =glGenerateMipmap= (rlgl only exposes it for 2D textures): the box reductions are separate single-level cubemaps rendered from each other, so no pass ever reads the image it writes. Cubemaps are allocated with every level down to 1 x 1 so they are mipmap-complete. rlgl state (framebuffer, viewport, matrices, blend, depth, culling, active texture unit) is restored after each step.

*** Ambient fallback and fog
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-ambient-fog
:END:
=lrg_env_active()= is false when no environment is bound, it is not baked, or IBL is disabled (=lrg_environment_set_ibl_enabled()=); the lit shader then uses the flat =ambientColor=. =lrg_environment_apply_to_light_set()= copies the ambient colour (scaled in linear space by its intensity and the exposure) and the distance fog (=lrg_light_set_set_fog()=, =lrg_light_set_set_fog_height()=, or =lrg_light_set_clear_fog()=) into a light set. The volumetric parameters feed [[file:volumetric-fog.org][LrgVolumetricFog]] (=lrg_volumetric_fog_set_from_environment()=); the =post_stack= reference names the =.rpost= file of the scene's [[file:../postprocess/post-stack.org][post stack]], which the caller loads.

*** Reflection probes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-probes
:END:
Probes added with =lrg_environment_add_probe()= are blended into =lrg_env_specular()=. On each bind the environment selects, among the probes that are captured and contain the reference point (=lrg_environment_set_reference_point()=), the two with the largest influence (ties: nearest centre) - =lrg_environment_select_probes()= does the same on the CPU. In the shader each probe's influence is evaluated at the shaded point (passed by the light model with =lrg_env_set_point()=, else the reference point), the probes' box-projected lookups are summed by weight (normalized when the weights exceed 1) and the global specular fills the remaining weight. Probe boxes are uploaded relative to the environment's floating origin (=lrg_environment_set_origin()=, use the light set's =lrg_light_set_get_origin()=).

*** Lit-shader hook and texture units
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-hook
:END:
=LRG_ENV_GLSL= (private header =lighting/lrg-environment-glsl-private.h=) is pasted into the light set's GLSL library, so user shaders built on =lrg_light_set_get_glsl_library()= get it too. It defines =lrg_env_active()=, =lrg_env_diffuse()=, =lrg_env_specular()=, =lrg_env_brdf()= and =lrg_env_set_point()=. Its binder, called by =lrg_light_set_apply()=, claims texture units from the light set's hook window (=lrg_light_set_hook_unit_first()=, =LRG_LIGHT_SET_HOOK_UNIT=): the prefiltered map always (every cube sampler of the hook points at it while inactive, so no =samplerCube= ever shares a unit with a =sampler2D=), then the BRDF table and two probe cubemaps when the window has room (at most =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_TEXTURE_UNITS=). On a driver with only 16 units only the prefiltered map is claimed: analytic BRDF, no probes.

    g_warning ("bake: %s", error->message);

    BeginMode3D (camera);
    lrg_environment_draw_sky (env, &(GrlVector3){ camera.position.x, camera.position.y,

    lrg_light_set_apply (lights, camera.position.x, camera.position.y, camera.position.z);
    /* ... draw the world with lrg_light_set_get_shader (lights) ... */
    EndMode3D ();
    EndDrawing ();
}
lrg_environment_release_gpu (env);   /* before CloseWindow() */

** Performance
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-performance
:END:
- *Bake cost* (resolution N): CPU face evaluation 6 N^2 lookups (128: about 100k, a few ms; equirect sampling dominates), SH projection the same, BRDF table 64 x 64 x 256 samples (about 1M GGX samples, 10-20 ms once per bake), GPU box chain (about 8 N^2 texels) and the prefilter (about 8 N^2 texels x 64 samples x 2 taps: 128 is about 17M texture fetches, well under a millisecond on a discrete GPU, about a second on llvmpipe). A dome bake adds six face read-backs (a pipeline stall). Bake at load time or on time-of-day steps, never every frame.
- *Memory*: radiance N^2 x 6 x 8 bytes (128: 0.8 MB), prefiltered with mips 4/3 of that (1 MB), BRDF table 48 KB; transient chain about 1/3 of the radiance plus a zero-filled upload buffer during the bake.
- *Per draw*: 9 vec3 uniforms and one cube fetch for diffuse + specular, one 2D fetch for the BRDF; each probe adds a box intersection and a cube fetch for fragments inside its box.
- *Binder*: uniform locations are cached per program (and re-validated against one location every bind); probe selection is a linear scan over the probes.

** Pitfalls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-pitfalls
:END:
- *Bake needs a context*: =lrg_environment_bake()= fails with =LRG_ENVIRONMENT_ERROR_GPU= before =InitWindow()=; release the maps (=lrg_environment_release_gpu()=) before =CloseWindow()=.
- *Load the images*: setting =sky.path= (or loading a level) does not read the image; call =lrg_environment_load_sources()= before baking, or the bake falls back to the gradient with a warning.
- *sRGB vs linear*: sky colours are sRGB like all engine colours; =.hdr= pixels are linear. A colour sky of (0.5, 0.5, 0.5) lights a white surface with linear 0.218, which displays as 0.5 again.
- *Dome rotation*: =rotation= is ignored for a dome source (the dome positions its sun itself); turn the dome instead.
- *Units*: the binder points its cube samplers at its own unit even when inactive; never set =lrgEnv*= sampler uniforms by hand.
- *Probes need the point*: user shaders built on the light set's GLSL library should call =lrg_env_set_point(p)= before =lrg_env_specular()= (=lrg_light_pbr()= does), else the reference point decides the probe weights for the whole draw.
- *Do not delete renderbuffers with rlUnloadTexture()*: the capture code relies on =rlUnloadFramebuffer()= to free its depth renderbuffer; deleting the renderbuffer id as a texture frees an unrelated texture of the same number (the default white texture, typically).

** Using it from the editor
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: environment-editor
:END:
The level being edited owns its environment (=lrg_level_get_environment()=, created on demand with =lrg_level_ensure_environment()=) and it is saved in the =.rlevel= =environment:= block. Wave 2b of 0.9.0 adds the editor's environment page (sky source and images with =guid:= pickers, exposure/rotation sliders that update live, ambient, fog, volumetric and post-stack fields, a "Bake" button that calls =lrg_environment_bake()=) and draws the sky and the IBL in the viewport through =LrgWorld3DRenderer=. Until then, edit the block in the =.rlevel= file; unknown keys survive editor saves.
