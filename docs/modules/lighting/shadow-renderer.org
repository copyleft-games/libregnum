* LrgShadowRenderer - 3D Shadow Maps
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lrgshadowrenderer---3d-shadow-maps
:END:
Shadow maps for [[file:../graphics/light-set.org][LrgLightSet]]: cascaded shadow maps for the directional light (the sun or moon), cube shadow maps for a budget of point lights (a torch, spell lights, key lights in a dungeon) and, since 0.9.0, perspective shadow maps for a budget of spot lights (a flashlight, a street lamp, a stage light). The planning is headless (splits, light matrices, which point and spot lights get a map); the passes and the three atlases need GL.

** Types
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: types
:END:
| Type | Kind | Notes |
|------+------+-------|
| =LrgShadowRenderer= | final GObject | settings, the frame's plan, the atlases, the caster shader |
| =LrgShadowPassKind= | enum (=none=, =cascade=, =cube-face=, =spot=) | what a pass renders |

Constants: =LRG_SHADOW_MAX_CASCADES= (4), =LRG_SHADOW_MAX_POINT_LIGHTS= (8), =LRG_SHADOW_MAX_SPOT_LIGHTS= (4), =LRG_SHADOW_NO_SLOT= (-1), =LRG_SHADOW_SPOT_UNIT= (11), =LRG_SHADOW_SUN_UNIT= (12) and =LRG_SHADOW_POINT_UNIT= (13), the texture units of the three atlases while a light set draws.

:END:

Matrix helpers (column-major 4 x 4 like OpenGL and raylib's =Matrix=): =lrg_shadow_look_at()=, =lrg_shadow_ortho()=, =lrg_shadow_perspective()=, =lrg_shadow_multiply()=, =lrg_shadow_transform_point()=, =lrg_shadow_split_distances()=, =lrg_shadow_cube_face_matrices()=, =lrg_shadow_spot_matrices()= (0.9.0: a spot's look-at and a perspective projection of twice the outer half-angle plus two degrees, near =max (radius / 500, 0.02)=, far the radius).

** Semantics
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: semantics
:END:
*** Cascades
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: cascades
:END:
The view from =near= to the shadow distance is split by the practical split scheme: split /i/ of /N/ is =lambda * n (f/n)^(i/N) + (1 - lambda) (n + (f - n) i/N)=, so near cascades are small and sharp. Each slice's eight corners get a bounding sphere (its radius rounded up to 1/16 so it does not breathe while the camera turns); an orthographic camera looks along the light at the sphere's centre from =radius + max (2 radius, 60)= behind it, so tall casters outside the view still throw their shadow into it. The projection is then moved so the draw-space origin lands on a texel corner: every point keeps its texel while the camera moves by whole texels, and shadow edges do not crawl. A fragment uses the first cascade whose box holds it (with a margin of the filter's reach), so there is no view-depth uniform.

*** Point lights
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: point-lights
:END:
A light competes for a cube when its shadow priority (=lrg_light_set_set_point_light_shadow()=) is above 0: highest priority first, then the smallest distance from the camera to its sphere, then the lowest id, up to the budget and within the =max_distance= reach. Each cube is six 90 degree faces (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z; up is +Y except +Z for the Y faces) that store the distance to the light over its radius. The light shader finds the face and its coordinates with the same axes (=LRG_SHADOW_GLSL=; =tests/test-shadow-renderer.c= checks the two agree).

*** Spot lights (0.9.0)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: spot-lights
:END:
A spot light ([[file:../graphics/light-set.org::#spot-lights][lrg_light_set_add_spot_light()]]) with a shadow priority above 0 competes for a spot map under the same order as point lights (priority, then distance to its sphere, then id), within =set_spots()='s budget and reach; spots never take cubes and points never take spot maps.  Each picked spot costs one pass (=LRG_SHADOW_PASS_SPOT=): a perspective camera at the light looking along its direction, wide enough for the outer cone plus a two degree margin for the filter.  The caster writes the distance to the light over its radius (mode 1, like a cube face), so one caster shader serves all three kinds.  The spot atlas is one row of =max_lights= tiles of =res= texels.  In the light shader =lrg_spot_shadow (slot, p, n)= pushes the fragment along its normal by =normal_offset= texels (the texel's world size at that distance from the cone's tangent), projects it into the tile and runs the same percentage-closer filter as the other maps (=set_filter()=, taps clamped to the tile), with the light's own bias or the renderer's.  =get_pass_state()= reports mode 1 and the light's position and radius during a spot pass.

*** Atlases and passes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: atlases-and-passes
:END:
Each atlas is a 32-bit float colour texture with a depth renderbuffer on a framebuffer of its own: the sun atlas holds the cascades two per row, the point atlas three by two faces per light, one light above the other, the spot atlas one tile per spot in a row. =begin_pass()= clears an atlas at its first pass of the frame, sets the tile's viewport, loads the pass's view and projection into rlgl (so =DrawMesh()= and any frustum culling that reads rlgl's matrices follow the light), turns depth testing on and face culling off (one-sided models cast from both sides) and points the caster shader at the pass. =end_pass()= flushes and restores the screen as =EndTextureMode()= does. Call the passes outside =BeginMode3D()= and any texture mode, with the screen as the target.

The caster shader uses raylib's attribute and uniform names; texels whose alpha is below 0.5 cast nothing (cut-out foliage, fences).

*** The pass cache
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: the-pass-cache
:END:
Each tile is cleared on its own (a scissored clear), so a tile keeps what it holds until its pass is rendered again.  With =set_refresh (frames)= above 0, =update()= marks a pass /current/ when its view-projection and light are exactly what its tile was rendered with and it is younger than =frames= updates; the game skips current passes (=pass_is_current()=) and =is_ready()= counts them as rendered.  Still key lights and a still camera then cost nothing; moving casters show within =frames= frames; =invalidate()= forgets every tile when the casters change (a new space), and a new atlas forgets them too.

*Kept until something changes.*  =set_refresh (LRG_SHADOW_REFRESH_NEVER)= never ages a tile out: a pass is re-rendered only when its view-projection or light changes or its tile is forgotten.  =lrg_shadow_renderer_invalidate_box (min, max)= forgets exactly the tiles whose light camera (as the tile was rendered) sees part of an axis-aligned box in draw space: a door that swung, a crate that fell, a character walking past a torch.  Name the space the caster left and the space it entered (one box around both); the faces of torches that cannot see it, and cascades that do not cover it, stay cached.  The test is the usual conservative one: a tile is kept only when all eight corners lie beyond one of its six clip planes (linear in the world point, so it holds for the cube faces' perspective as well as the cascades' orthographic projection).  An unordered box, or one with a NaN, forgets every tile; the call returns how many it forgot.  Casters animated in their vertex shader (swaying billboards, grass) keep the pose of the frame their tile was drawn in until the tile is re-rendered, so pair them with an age-based refresh, or accept frozen sway in still lights' shadows.  The cascades follow the camera and are texel-snapped, so a moving camera re-renders them every frame whatever the refresh; the cache pays for still torches and key lights, and for every pass while the camera stands still.  =tests/test-shadow-renderer.c= =/shadow/invalidate-box=: two torches under a sun, 500 still updates with every pass current, a box beside one torch forgetting some of its faces and none of the far torch's, and a NaN box forgetting all.

*Staggering.*  The passes of a still view all come due on the same frame (at midnight the moon's cascades and the torch's six faces).  A pass that is only stale by age is therefore rationed: =update()= lets at most =ceil (passes / frames)= of them render per frame, the oldest first, and the others keep their tile until they are next in line, never past twice the refresh.  A pass whose view or light changed always renders.  =lrg_shadow_renderer_mark_rendered (pass)= records a pass as rendered (what =end_pass()= does) for games that fill a tile by other means and for the headless test of the cache and its staggering (=tests/test-shadow-renderer.c=, =/shadow/stagger=: nine passes, refresh 4, at most three a frame, none waiting past twice the refresh, a moved camera re-rendering its cascades at once).

*Casters with their own program.*  =get_pass_state()= gives the open pass's mode (0 cascade, 1 cube face), the cube's light position and radius and the way a cascade's light travels, so a caster whose geometry is built on the GPU (=lrg_billboard_batch_draw_shadow()=: upright quads facing the light, alpha cut out) writes the caster shader's depth or distance itself.

*Terrain casters.*  =lrg_terrain_draw()= with the caster shader costs, per pass, a culling test per 4 x 4 block of chunks (and per chunk only in blocks that can be seen), one program bind and per visible chunk three matrix uniforms and one base-vertex draw per mesh; no VAO switches inside a page.  The caster shader only reads the position, and the driver fetches only the attributes a program reads, so the terrain's shared layout needs no separate depth-only vertex format.  Pixel identity of the atlas with the per-mesh =DrawMesh()= path is tested in =tests/test-terrain-render.c= ([[file:../graphics/terrain.org::#gpu-layout][terrain GPU layout]]).

*** Lookups
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lookups
:END:
A light set with a renderer (=lrg_light_set_set_shadow_renderer()=) binds the atlases in =lrg_light_set_apply()= once the renderer =is_ready()=, and its shader multiplies the directional light's term by the cascade lookup, each shadowed point light by its cube lookup and each shadowed spot light by its spot lookup (=pointShadowBias[k]= and =spotShadowInfo[k].x= carry per-light biases, 0 for the renderer's). Before the lookup the fragment is pushed along its normal by =normal_offset= texels (against acne on slopes) and its depth reduced by the depth bias (in world units, converted per cascade). =strength= mixes the filtered result toward lit. Billboards (=LrgBillboardBatch=) are lit without shadows.

** Example
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example
:END:
#+begin_src C
g_autoptr(LrgShadowRenderer) shadows = lrg_shadow_renderer_new ();
guint pass;

lrg_shadow_renderer_set_sun (shadows, 2048, 3, 120.0f, 0.6f);
lrg_shadow_renderer_set_points (shadows, 512, 2, 40.0f);
lrg_shadow_renderer_set_spots (shadows, 1024, 1, 60.0f);   /* the player's flashlight */
lrg_light_set_set_point_light_shadow (lights, flashlight, 20);
lrg_shadow_renderer_set_refresh (shadows, LRG_SHADOW_REFRESH_NEVER);
lrg_light_set_set_point_light_shadow (lights, torch, 10);
lrg_light_set_set_shadow_renderer (lights, shadows);

/* each frame, before BeginMode3D (): what moved (in draw space) */
lrg_shadow_renderer_invalidate_box (shadows, door_min, door_max);
lrg_shadow_renderer_update (shadows, lights, eye, forward, up, 65.0f, aspect, 0.1f);
for (pass = 0; pass < lrg_shadow_renderer_get_pass_count (shadows); pass++)
{
    /* with a refresh set: a current pass's tile is still good */
    if (lrg_shadow_renderer_pass_is_current (shadows, pass))
        continue;
    if (!lrg_shadow_renderer_begin_pass (shadows, pass))
        break;
    lrg_static_batch_set_shader (world, lrg_shadow_renderer_get_caster_shader (shadows));
    lrg_static_batch_draw (world, LRG_STATIC_BATCH_ALL_LAYERS);
    lrg_shadow_renderer_end_pass (shadows);
}
lrg_static_batch_set_shader (world, lrg_light_set_get_shader (lights));
/* then BeginMode3D (), lrg_light_set_apply (), draw as usual */
#+end_src

** Tests
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: tests
:END:
=tests/test-shadow-renderer.c=: the matrix helpers, the split scheme, every slice corner inside its cascade with a floating origin, texel snapping, the cube faces against the shader's lookup, the point plan (priority, budget, reach, removal). =tests/test-light-set.c= =/light-set/shader-shadows=: the sun and a point light behind an occluder darken the floor under it in a hidden window, the open floor stays lit, strength 0 removes the shadow. =tests/test-pbr.c= =/pbr/spot-shadow-plan= (spot budget and priority, spots kept apart from cubes, pass order, the spot camera) and =/pbr/gpu/spot-shadow= (a shadowed spot above an occluder darkens the floor under it and lights the floor beside it).

** Render layers
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: render-layers
:END:
=lrg_shadow_renderer_set_caster_mask()= / =lrg_shadow_renderer_get_caster_mask()= choose which render layers cast (all by default). =lrg_shadow_renderer_begin_pass()= installs the caster mask as the active mask and =lrg_shadow_renderer_end_pass()= restores the previous one, so layer-aware casters (static batch chunks, terrain chunks, billboard batches) on other layers draw nothing in the pass. Changing the mask drops the pass cache. See [[file:../graphics/render-layers.org][Render layers]].

** Pitfalls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pitfalls
:END:
- A spot's shadow priority is set with =lrg_light_set_set_point_light_shadow()= (spots are point lights with a cone); without =set_spots (..., n > 0, ...)= it never gets a map.
- Spot maps cover the outer cone only: a light whose cone is wider than about 85 degrees half-angle loses resolution fast; use a point light with a cube instead.
- Peter-panning (shadows detached from their casters) means the bias is too large for that light; acne means too small.  Tune it per light with =lrg_light_set_set_light_shadow_bias()= or the light component's =shadow-bias=.

** Using it from the editor
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: editor
:END:
A light node's =cast_shadows= and =shadow_bias= params reach the light component (=cast-shadows=, =shadow-bias=); the world renderer gives cast-shadow lights priority 1 and plans the passes.
