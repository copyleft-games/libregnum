** Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: overview
:END:
=LrgParticleRibbonRenderer= (=particles/lrg-particle-ribbon-renderer.h=, 0.9.0) draws a particle system as camera-facing strips. In /trail/ mode every particle drags the path it flew (sparks, tracers, smoke trails behind rockets, sword swooshes); in /connect/ mode one ribbon runs through all live particles in spawn order (beams, magic streams, lightning, tentacles). Width varies along the strip with a curve, colour with a gradient, and the texture's u coordinate stretches, tiles or repeats per segment. Geometry is built headless by =lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_build()=, which tests check; =draw()= submits exactly those quads through rlgl. The =ribbon= renderer kind of a [[file:particle-asset.org][.rparticles]] emitter creates and configures one.

** Concepts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: concepts
:END:
- *Trail history*: =lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_update()= (called after every system step; an =LrgParticleEffect= does it) records, per particle id (=LrgParticle.id=), a point whenever the particle travelled =min-distance= since its last one, with the particle's width (=width= x size) and colour at that moment. Points age; points older than =trail-lifetime= are dropped, and a trail keeps at most =max-points= (oldest dropped first). The live particle itself is the trail's /head/, added at build time at its current position, so a trail never lags. When a particle dies its trail stays and fades out point by point until the last one expires.
- *Connect mode* needs no history: build sorts the live particles by id, newest first, and strings them together (head = newest particle).
- *Strip geometry*: for each strip point the tangent is the direction between its neighbours, the side vector is =normalize (cross (tangent, eye - point))= scaled to half its width (times =width-curve= at the point's position =f= along the strip, 0 head .. 1 tail); a degenerate side (looking straight along the strip) keeps the previous one. Consecutive points make a quad; points closer than 1e-5 are merged.
- *Colour*: the point's colour times =color-gradient= at =f=.
- *UVs*: =v= runs 0 .. 1 across. =u= is =f= with =STRETCH=, distance-from-head / =uv-tile-length= with =TILE=, and 0 .. 1 per quad with =PER_SEGMENT=.
- *Default texture*: without a texture, a generated 4 x 32 texture that is opaque along the centre line and fades to the edges (bilinear), which reads as a soft streak.

** Example
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example
:END:
#+begin_src C
g_autoptr(LrgParticleRibbonRenderer) trails = lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_new ();
g_autoptr(LrgKeyframeCurve)          taper = lrg_keyframe_curve_new ();
g_autoptr(LrgColorGradient)          fade = lrg_color_gradient_new_from_string ("0 #ffffffff; 1 #ff400000", NULL);
gfloat                               eye[3];

lrg_keyframe_curve_add_key (taper, 0.0f, 1.0f, LRG_EASING_LINEAR);
lrg_keyframe_curve_add_key (taper, 1.0f, 0.0f, LRG_EASING_LINEAR);
lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_set_width (trails, 0.05f);
lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_set_width_curve (trails, taper);
lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_set_color_gradient (trails, fade);
lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_set_trail_lifetime (trails, 0.15f);

/* each frame */
lrg_particle_system_update (sparks, dt);
lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_update (trails, sparks, dt);
BeginMode3D (camera);
lrg_particle_ribbon_renderer_draw (trails, sparks, eye);
EndMode3D ();
#+end_src

In a =.rparticles= file the same is =renderer: {kind: ribbon, width: 0.05, width-curve: "0 1; 1 0", color-gradient: "0 #ffffffff; 1 #ff400000", trail-lifetime: 0.15}=.

** Performance
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: performance
:END:
On an i7-1280P core (=taskset -c 2=): 1 480 trails holding 39 400 points build 37 000 quads in 6.2 ms; recording 1 000 particles costs about 0.3 ms per step on top of their simulation. Cost is linear in points: =max-points= and =min-distance= are the levers. Drawing is one rlgl batch (flushed as it fills).

** Pitfalls
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pitfalls
:END:
- Call =update()= after every system step; without it trail mode shows only heads (no strip). =LrgParticleEffect= does it for you.
- The renderer is per system and keyed by particle id: do not share one between systems.
- Connect mode strings particles in spawn order: a burst of particles flying apart makes a zigzag. Use it with a steady rate and coherent motion.
- Trails outlive particles by up to =trail-lifetime=: an effect is not "finished" while a trail is still fading (=lrg_particle_effect_is_alive()= counts them).
