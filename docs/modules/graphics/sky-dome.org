* LrgSkyDome - Camera-Centred Sky
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lrgskydome---camera-centred-sky
:END:
A sky drawn around the camera before the world: a colour gradient sphere (ground → horizon → zenith), an optional textured horizon band (one panorama wrapped all the way round, or two halves such as the east/west sky images of a classic RPG), a sun disc with a soft halo, and a time-of-day helper that interpolates a small, configurable set of colour keyframes and moves the sun along its daily arc.

The gradient geometry, colours, keyframe interpolation and band mapping are computed headlessly and exposed for tests; only =draw()= needs GL. Drawing goes through rlgl's immediate batch, so the sky owns no GPU objects.

** Semantics
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: semantics
:END:
*** Directions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: directions
:END:
Azimuth is measured in degrees clockwise seen from above: 0 is -Z, 90 is +X, 180 is +Z, 270 is -X. Elevation is the angle above the horizontal plane.

*** Gradient
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: gradient
:END:
For a direction with unit Y component =y=, the colour is =lerp(horizon, zenith, y)= above the horizon and =lerp(horizon, ground, -y)= below; the ground colour follows the horizon unless set. Interpolation is per RGBA channel, rounded to nearest. =get_color_at()= returns exactly what the dome vertices get.

The sphere has =2 * rings + 1= latitude rows from the nadir to the zenith, one exactly on the horizon, each with =segments + 1= vertices (seam duplicated) at the dome radius, relative to the camera. Triangles are counter-clockwise seen from the centre. Defaults: 32 segments, 8 rings, radius 100.

*** Horizon band
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: horizon-band
:END:
- The band is a vertical cylinder at 0.9 × radius between two elevations (default -5° to 40°; a cylindrical panorama keeps vertical lines straight). The texture's top row is at the top.
- =set_band_texture()= wraps one texture once round the horizon: =u = (azimuth - heading) / 360=.
- =set_band_halves()= wraps two textures, each over 180°: the first over =[heading, heading + 180)=, the second over the rest, each with =u= from 0 to 1. For example Daggerfall's 512 × 220 sky halves: pass them in the order they join left to right as the viewer turns right.
- =u= increases with azimuth, so the image reads left to right as the viewer turns right (not mirrored).
- =get_band_uv()= says which texture and which =u= face an azimuth. The band tint multiplies the textures (e.g. to darken them at night, or swap textures per time of day).

*** Sun
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: sun
:END:
A disc of angular radius =sun_size= (default 2°, 0 hides it) in the sun colour, over a halo four times larger fading from 40 % to 0 alpha. The sun colour's alpha scales both, and the sun is skipped once its halo is entirely below the horizon. =get_sun_direction()= is the unit direction towards the sun; negate it for a directional light.

*** Time of day
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: time-of-day
:END:
- Keyframes hold zenith, horizon and sun colours at an hour in =[0, 24)=, kept sorted; adding one at an existing hour replaces it. The defaults are midnight (dark blue, sun alpha 0), dawn at 6 (orange horizon), noon (blue) and dusk at 18. =clear_keyframes()= removes them.
- =set_time_of_day (hours)= wraps =hours= into =[0, 24)=, interpolates linearly between the keyframe at or before it and the next one, cyclically across midnight (one keyframe is constant; none leaves the colours alone), and moves the sun: with =θ = (hours - 6) / 24 × 2π= and tilt =τ= (default 30°) the direction is =(cos θ, sin θ cos τ, sin θ sin τ)= - rising at +X at 6, highest at noon leaning towards +Z, setting at -X at 18, below the horizon at night.
- Set colours or the sun direction directly afterwards to override.

*** Drawing
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: drawing
:END:
=draw (camera_position)= flushes the batch, disables depth test, depth writes and back-face culling, draws the gradient, the sun, then the band (so the band's scenery hides a setting sun), flushes, and re-enables all three (raylib's defaults). Call it first inside =BeginMode3D()=, with the camera's eye position; the radius only has to lie inside the far plane.

** Example
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: example
:END:
#+begin_src C
g_autoptr(LrgSkyDome) sky = lrg_sky_dome_new ();
GrlVector3 sun;

lrg_sky_dome_set_band_halves (sky, sky_east, sky_west);
lrg_sky_dome_set_band_range (sky, -2.0f, 30.0f);

/* each frame */
lrg_sky_dome_set_time_of_day (sky, game_hours);
lrg_sky_dome_get_sun_direction (sky, &sun);

grl_camera3d_begin (camera);
lrg_sky_dome_draw (sky, &camera_position);
/* ... terrain, static batches, billboards ... */
grl_camera3d_end (camera);
#+end_src
