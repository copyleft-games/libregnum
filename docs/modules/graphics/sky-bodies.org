* LrgSkyBodies
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lrg-sky-bodies
:END:
=LrgSkyBodies= (=src/graphics/lrg-sky-bodies.h=, final) draws what a sky shows besides its gradient: a deterministic star field that turns with the sky and fades in at night, up to =LRG_SKY_BODIES_MAX_MOONS= (4) moon discs lit by their phase from the side facing the sun, and an optional sun disc with a halo.  It is camera-centred like [[file:sky-dome.org][LrgSkyDome]] and drawn right after it, or after a painted band to put real bodies over painted art.  The maths lives in =LrgCelestial= (see [[file:../weather/index.org][the weather module]]); this type only draws.

** Semantics
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: semantics
:END:
- *Draw order*: stars (additive blend), the sun's halo and disc, then the moons; depth test and writes off, culling off, the immediate rlgl batch flushed around it.  Bodies whose disc is wholly below the horizon are skipped; stars below the horizon are skipped and low ones dimmed (=min (1, 6 y + 0.2)=).
- *Moons*: the dark side is drawn first in the dark colour (default near-black at alpha 230, scaled by the moon's alpha), so stars do not shine through a new moon; then the lit part as rows between the limb and the terminator (=lrg_celestial_moon_disc_span()=), with the disc's +x axis turned towards the sun's direction projected on the disc, so a crescent's horns point away from the sun.  The sun direction set with =set_sun()= is used even when the disc is hidden (size 0).
- *Stars*: =set_stars (count, seed)= generates directions over the whole sphere once (=lrg_celestial_generate_stars()=, the same on every platform); =set_star_rotation (angle, tilt)= turns them about the orbit normal (=lrg_celestial_rotate_sky()=), so a game turning them by the sun's orbit angle plus a yearly drift shows other constellations by season.  Brightness 0.15 .. 1 scales size and level; visibility 0 skips the stars entirely.
- Headless: everything but =draw()= runs without a window (tests).
